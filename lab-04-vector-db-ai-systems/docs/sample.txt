Lab 04 introduces the vector data layer for AI systems on AWS.

Key idea:
- Convert text into embeddings
- Store vectors in OpenSearch
- Perform similarity search to retrieve relevant chunks

Why it matters:
LLMs are stateless and cannot remember documents. Vector search creates a searchable memory layer for retrieval-augmented generation.

Components:
- Amazon Bedrock embeddings
- OpenSearch vector index
- Lambda ingestion and search APIs

Outcome:
You can ingest content and retrieve the most relevant chunks for a query.
